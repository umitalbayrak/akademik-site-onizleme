About

Dr. Ümit Albayrak is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Technologies at Ilgın Vocational School, Selçuk University. His research in computer engineering focuses on developing, applying and evaluating artificial intelligence, deep learning, computer vision and data-driven methods across different problem domains.

His work centres on deep-learning-based image processing and classification, computer vision applications, machine learning methods and the performance analysis of AI models. He studies classification approaches on different image types and datasets, and the relationships between data collection processes, imaging conditions, model selection and experimental evaluation. Agricultural image analysis and plant disease detection are among the application areas of this work.

He also works on natural language processing, sentiment analysis, educational technology and learning analytics. His approach emphasises applying AI methods to real-world problems, comparative evaluation of experimental results and the reproducibility of research.
